featured case study

How Axiom Education
found their voice.

Client Axiom Education Education · Teacher-led
Problem

A trusted enrichment brand with great outcomes, but a social presence that wasn't moving the needle. Content felt generic, the filming process was painful, the teachers' stories were nowhere to be seen — and none of it was making much impact on the page.

Approach

We rebuilt their brand on social from the inside out — teacher-led storytelling, a consistent visual identity system, and content designed to speak directly to the students they actually teach. Production became effortless, not exhausting.

Result

The right students started showing up — pre-sold by the content they'd watched. The brand finally felt like itself on social, and the teachers became someone their audience recognised and trusted. They doubled their enrolments after first year, and opened their second branch after second year of engaging us.

"Everything was planned, very organised, very structured. They are always open to new ideas. The results were very good. Most importantly, it's the right target segment that I'm looking for."

Ashley T. Founder, Axiom Education
